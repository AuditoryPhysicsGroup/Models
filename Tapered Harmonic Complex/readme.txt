Software and audio example accompany the manuscript
Altoè A., "Suppressing spectral edge effectsin Schroeder's Harmonic Complex" (2026)

the script "compare_schroeder_tapered" gives an example on how to use the software
to design tapered schroeder'complexes and related stimuli, with constant(ish) envelope
and frequency linearly increasing over time

the script "example_log_sweep" shows how to use the proposed method to design
periodic schroeder's-like low-crest factor signal, but where the frqeuency changes exponentially with time

Finally, "example_schroeder_phase_complex" shows how to use the proposed method to
improve the design of  "Schroeder's phase complexes" stimuli originally proposed by 
 Lentz and Leek (2001) Journal of the Association for Research in Otolaryngology 2.4 pp 408-422.

The folder Audio examples present a few examples of slowly modulated schroeder/sweep-like stimuli,
to appreciate their not-so-sublted perceptual differences
